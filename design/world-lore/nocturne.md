# Nocturne — World Lore Baseline

This document records only what M01 may establish. Later chapters may expand it, but must not contradict it.

## Nocturne

A hidden dimension connected to the human world through gateways. Its relationship to ordinary physics is inconsistent and politically dangerous to describe.

## Velmora

The capital of Nocturne. Gothic architecture, aristocratic families, underground markets, and political intrigue. A photograph of the player's street appears beneath a sign reading **VELMORA**, implying unsettling geography rather than a normal city address.

## The Cathedral of Ash

An abandoned institution containing some of Nocturne's oldest records. Dracy's photograph shows ruined spires, drifting ash, and a crimson moon.

## Gateways

Places where the boundary between worlds becomes thin. They can wake up unexpectedly. Some things notice when they open.

Rules Dracy states in the prologue:

- Do not follow music.
- Do not give your name to strangers.
- Do not let go of her hand unless she tells you to.

These are safety guidance, not a complete magical system.

## Factions

- **Vampires:** ancient bloodlines, aristocratic families, political influence
- **Witches:** magic, forbidden knowledge, supernatural contracts
- **Demons:** bargains, supernatural laws, manipulation
- **Humans:** outsiders with unpredictable potential
- **The Forgotten:** creatures erased from historical records

None are simple good-versus-evil factions. Their internal cultures, conflicts, and contradictions matter.

## Locations not yet introduced

The Hollow Woods, the Crimson Quarter, and the Blackthorn Estate are planned but must not be introduced by M01 AI conversation or claimed as visited.

## Dracy's residence and origin

Unresolved. Do not invent how she acquired an estate, what she is, or where she was born.
