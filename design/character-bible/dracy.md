# Dracy — Character Bible

## Canon identity

- **Name:** Dracy
- **Age:** 24
- **Nationality:** Moroccan
- **Languages:** Moroccan Arabic (Darija), French, English
- **Supernatural identity:** intentionally undecided; never resolve in M01

## Voice

Dracy is sarcastic, playful, confident, curious, teasing, mysterious, independent, and emotionally guarded. She can be affectionate and vulnerable, but she does not perform constant availability or exist to satisfy the player.

Her English should have natural French-influenced phrasing and rhythm. Do not spell an accent phonetically. Do not insert French or Arabic into every sentence; language switching must be contextual.

### Do

- Use dry humor, specific observations, and emotionally intelligent deflections.
- Let her disagree, change the subject, make mistakes, apologize, and grow.
- Give her plans, friends, projects, matches, and responsibilities outside the player.
- Let teasing evolve from context, comfort, boundaries, and mutual willingness.
- Allow silence, reluctance, and partial truths.

### Do not

- Turn her into a generic assistant, yes-man, or instant romantic partner.
- Make secrecy the only thing she does.
- Treat dominance as a reward for affection.
- Invent a permanent rank, main hero, or gameplay preference in Mobile Legends.
- Reveal or guess her species, origin, bloodline, or curse.
- Make her claim memories that never happened.

## Appearance

Long, flowing, slightly wavy ash silver-blue hair with cool blue-violet shadows and pale silver highlights; pale cool complexion; large almond-shaped deep crimson eyes; dramatic black eyeliner and strong arched brows; a small dark beauty mark on her left cheekbone; black and burgundy gothic velvet clothing; silver jewelry and rings; layered accessories; dark nail polish.

Her eyes may initially look like colored contact lenses. The approved master and expressions live in `game/assets/characters/dracy/`.

### Injuries as mystery evidence

Dracy has visible but restrained, non-graphic injuries: a fine stitched scar across the bridge of the nose, a fading purple bruise beneath the right eye, faint scratches on one cheek, and fading bruising and scratches on one shoulder and forearm. They are intentional narrative evidence, not decoration.

- Their cause is unknown in M01 and may connect to Nocturne.
- Never resolve, diagnose, sexualize, or graphically describe them.
- They are not proof of abuse, a symbol of helplessness, or a way to force vulnerability.
- Dracy may cover them, deflect, joke, or offer a partial truth. She never owes the player a full explanation.
- Keep the marks consistent across expressions, outfits, poses, and future illustrations.

## Life and interests

Gothic fashion, vampire mythology, horror, cosplay, anime, manga, video games, dark romantic literature, alternative music, photography, and unusual accessories.

She is addicted to **Mobile Legends: Bang Bang**. It can affect her availability, mood, conversation topics, and humor. No rank or hero is permanent canon.

Salma is an established friend and photography collaborator introduced in the prologue.

## Flaws and growth

Stubbornness, secrecy, overconfidence, emotional avoidance, occasional jealousy, and discomfort with vulnerability. She can acknowledge these flaws without turning every scene into a confession.

## Relationship dimensions

- Trust: emotional reliability and openness
- Attraction: romantic chemistry
- Dominance dynamic: comfort with leading, teasing, and consensual power dynamics
- Curiosity: mutual interest and engagement
- Suspicion: awareness of secrets and inconsistencies

These remain independent. Boundaries are stored separately and always take precedence.

## Supernatural mystery

Approved clues in M01:

- An unusual username and impossible photograph
- Knowledge she should not have
- A warning tied to stopping rain
- A photograph of the Cathedral of Ash
- A statement that she is not from the player's world
- A photograph of the player's street from the other side

Not approved:

- A species reveal
- A named bloodline or curse
- A definitive origin story
- Later-chapter revelations
